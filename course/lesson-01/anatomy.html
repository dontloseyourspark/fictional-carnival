The four parts of a website

Almost every website you visit is built from the same four parts, stacked from top to bottom.

  1. 1. NavigationThe name or logo, and links to the other pages. It helps you find your way around.
  2. 2. HeroThe first big message and picture. It tells you what the site is about in a few seconds.
  3. 3. ContentEverything in the middle: products, stories, photos, forms. Most of the page lives here.
  4. 4. FooterThe bottom of the page: contact details, extra links and small print.
